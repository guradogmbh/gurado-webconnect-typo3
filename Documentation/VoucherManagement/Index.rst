===============================
Redemption, reports and finance
===============================

Redemption in-store
===================

Gift vouchers, gift cards and digital gift certificates sold online are
managed in gurado.
Businesses can redeem them in-store with the gurado webapp or supported
iOS/Android apps; compatible POS integrations are available depending
on the customer's configuration.

Free-plan in-store redemption is subject to a limit of **10 redemptions
in a 30-day period**. Paid plans are available for additional usage.

Payment methods
===============

On the Free plan, businesses can use invoice/bank transfer to receive
payment directly in their **own bank account** without a paid subscription.

Paid plans can connect supported providers such as PayPal, Stripe,
Worldline, Nexi, hobex and Mollie to automate online payment processing.
Provider transaction fees and regional availability depend on the setup.

Physical gift cards
===================

Eligible paid plans can offer physical gift cards during online ordering.
gurado handles production and shipping to the customer or recipient.
Printing and delivery charges may apply.

Accounting and reporting
========================

gurado centralizes visibility into voucher sales, redemptions and
outstanding voucher values. These records can assist an accounting team
with transaction reconciliation and outstanding-value monitoring.

Available report views, downloads, schedules and formats vary by
gurado module and configuration. Confirm the exact required export with
your gurado contact before relying on it for a specific accounting workflow.

For help, see https://support.gurado.de.
